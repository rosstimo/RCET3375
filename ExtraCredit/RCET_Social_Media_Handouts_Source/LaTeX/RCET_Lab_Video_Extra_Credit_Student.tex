\sectionbar{What to do}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.3em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=1pt]
  \item Record a short video while you are already working in RCET lab.
  \item Briefly say what you are working on or what the equipment/program/circuit is doing.
  \item Upload it. \textbf{No editing, music, graphics, or special effects are required.}
  \item Add the appropriate mentions and the standard hashtags below.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Examples:} ``We're programming a PIC microcontroller and trying to get this display working.'' \quad ``We're using the oscilloscope to troubleshoot this circuit.''

\textbf{Good platforms:} Instagram, Facebook, X, YouTube Shorts, or another instructor-approved platform you already use. You do \textbf{not} need to create a new social-media account.

\sectionbar{What do @mentions and \#hashtags do?}
\textbf{@mention/tag:} points to a specific account or page. It helps RCET, the College of Technology, or ISU find your post and potentially share it.\par
\textbf{\#hashtag:} labels the topic. People can click or search a hashtag to find related public posts.

# RCET3375 Extra Credit

RCET3375 has two optional ways to earn additional credit.

## Lab Mastery

Released lab assignments may include a **Mastery** challenge after the required work. Completing the Mastery challenge earns **+5 percentage points on that lab assignment**. That is equivalent to offsetting one day of the course's 5%-per-day late penalty.

Mastery is optional and does not replace any required part of the lab.

## Weekly social-media extra credit

Students may also earn up to **+5% on the current week's lab grade** through qualifying social-media posts that show authentic RCET lab work.
